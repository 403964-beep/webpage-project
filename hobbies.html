The Balance

Athletic Discipline Meets High-Energy Gaming

Whether on the football gridiron, the basketball court, or dropping into online matches with friends, my hobbies center on competition, quick decision-making, and teamwork. The physical discipline demanded by high school sports keeps me sharp across everything I do.

Beyond varsity preparation and school classes, I spend my free time playing Madden, Fortnite, and NBA 2K26, playing pickup basketball with teammates, studying opposing teams' game film, and working out at the gym.

Core Interests & Pastimes

The activities, sports, and favorite games that keep me energized and having fun every week.

Athletics

Football & Strength Conditioning

Dedicated year-round training with Grossmont High School football: early morning weight room sessions, agility ladder drills, sprint biomechanics, and studying game tape to sharpen defensive reads and spatial reaction speed.

Gaming

Video Games

Madden Fortnite NBA 2K26

I love playing video games with friends and competing online. My favorite games are Madden for running football plays and strategic head-to-head competition, Fortnite for squad matches and fast-paced battle royale action, and NBA 2K26 for high-tempo court play and green-meter shot timing.

Team Sports

Basketball & Pickup Runs

Hitting the courts with friends for competitive pickup basketball. Sharpening perimeter shooting, high-tempo fast-break decision making, perimeter defense, and quick lateral footwork that complements gridiron agility.

Football IQ

Studying Opposing Teams Film

Breaking down game tape on Hudl to analyze opponent formations, offensive and defensive tendencies, coverage weaknesses, and player habits. Developing high-level football IQ and anticipation on the field before kickoff.

Strength & Fitness

Working Out at the Gym

Dedicated gym training focused on weight lifting, compound strength movements, dumbbell circuits, and core stability. Prioritizing progressive overload, explosive power, and athletic recovery to stay physically dominant and injury-free.

Outreach

Peer Tutoring & Coding Clinics

Sharing enthusiasm for STEM by assisting classmates with algebra and introductory programming logic. Hosting casual weekend coding walk-throughs to help beginners build their first personal web page.
